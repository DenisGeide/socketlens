# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in SocketLens welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and professional.
- Assume good intent while still being clear.
- Keep feedback focused on the work.
- Respect maintainers' time and project scope.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language
- Public disclosure of private information
- Sustained disruption of project discussions
- Bad-faith security reports or spam

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or community.
